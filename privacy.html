Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Mala Loop is a mantra repetition app for iPhone, published by EAB Digital. It is designed so that your practice stays yours. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

We do not collect any data

Mala Loop has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party software development kits. The app makes no internet connections of its own. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage:

  • Audio files you import or record, and affirmations you type.
  • The groups, routines, and settings you create, including your reminder time and whether you have seen the introduction.
  • Your session history: the date, mantra name, group, count, and duration of each session.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this information is included in that backup under Apple's terms, like any other app's data. It is removed when you delete the app. You can also delete any individual item inside the app at any time.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you choose to record a mantra. Recordings are saved to the app's local storage and are never transmitted. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Microphone.

Spoken affirmations

Typed affirmations are read aloud using the speech voices built into iOS. The text is processed on your device by Apple's speech synthesis and is not sent to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. No notification service outside your iPhone is involved. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Files you share into the app

When you use "Copy to Mala Loop" from another app such as Voice Memos or Files, a copy of that audio file is placed in Mala Loop's local storage. The original file is not modified.

Purchases

Mala Loop is sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details, or any other personal information from the purchase.

Children

Mala Loop does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that handles data differently, such as optional cloud sync or purchasable content, this page will be updated before that version is released and the app's App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
