Support

Mala Loop repeats a mantra or affirmation for a chosen count or time, keeps your library organised in groups, and tracks your daily practice. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Contact

Common questions

How do I add a mantra?

In the library, tap Upload audio to pick a file, Record to use your voice, or Affirmation to type a sentence that the app will speak. You can also share a recording from Voice Memos and choose "Copy to Mala Loop".

Playback stops when I lock the screen

Playback is designed to continue with the screen locked. If it stops, check that Low Power Mode is off and that another app is not also playing audio. Start playback first, then lock. Lock-screen controls show play and pause.

The count is wrong after a phone call

A call, Siri, or an alarm pauses the session so the count stays accurate. Playback resumes automatically when iOS allows it, or tap play to continue.

How do I move a mantra to a different group?

Tap the three dots on the mantra row, then Move to, and choose a group. Use the three dots on a group header to add directly into that group, rename it, or reorder its items.

What is a routine?

A routine saves the selected mantra together with the count or timer, pause, and speed. Tap Save current under Routines in the player, give it a name, and start it later with one tap.

How does the streak work?

A day counts once you complete at least one repetition. The streak is the run of consecutive days ending today. It stays alive until midnight if you practised yesterday.

The daily reminder does not arrive

Open iOS Settings, Notifications, Mala Loop, and make sure notifications are allowed. Then turn the reminder off and on again in the app's Settings.

Can I back up or sync my library?

Your library is included in your normal iPhone backup. Syncing between devices is not available in this version.

Recording says microphone access is needed

Open iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone, and allow Mala Loop. Microphone audio is used only for recordings you choose to save.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items from their three-dots menu, delete sessions from the history list, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
